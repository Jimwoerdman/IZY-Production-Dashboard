Shutting down the printer

At the end of the day. For this printer it is simple: switch off and tidy up.

1

Switch off the printer

  1. Press the STOP button: the one with the red light around it, next to the machine door.
2

Tidy up everything around the printer

Then tidy up everything around the printer completely. The next person must be able to start straight away.

  • Printed and blank bottles back in their place
  • Boxes, sleeves and stickers put away
  • Used cloths and waste thrown away
  • Workspace and floor around the printer clear and clean
