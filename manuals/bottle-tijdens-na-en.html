IZY Bottles · Work instruction · Part 3

During and after printing

For Bottle 1 and Bottle 2. What to do while the printer is running, and how to log your work in the print dashboard afterwards. Do part 1 and 2 first.

Golden rule

Everything besides printing you do only while a bottle is in the printer. The printer should never wait for you.

After printing: log the print job

When to log?

Only log once the next order is already running. So: first the new bottle in the printer and printing, then log. Otherwise it costs print time.

  1. Open the print dashboard, Active Queue tab.
  2. Find the order you just finished and click Log print.
  3. In the pop-up, fill in:
    • Pieces printed this session: number of bottles printed
    • Faulty Prints: number of faulty bottles
    • Printer Used: Bottle 1 or Bottle 2
  4. Click Submit.

3

Does the order need sleeving?

After logging, an order that needs sleeves moves to To Sleeve automatically. After sleeving, process it in the dashboard too:

  1. Sleeve the bottles (while the next order is printing).
  2. Find the order in the dashboard and press the red ✕ Sleeve button.
  3. Confirm. The button turns green (✓ Sleeved) and the order is ready to ship.
