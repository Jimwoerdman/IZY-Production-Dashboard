Starting the mug printer

How to switch on the mug printer (Mug 1) and get it ready to print. It works slightly differently from the bottle printer.

You need: non-dust cloth (lint-free) · PC code: 1997

The machine

  • Button panel next to the catch tray: START (green), STOP (red), COLOR and WHITE.
  • Top: ink cartridges W K C M Y V with valves.
  • Computer with the DPCS Printer software.
1

Switch on the machine

  1. Press START on the machine.
  2. The green light comes on.
2

Switch on and log in to the computer

  1. Switch on the computer with power on.
  2. Log in with code 1997.
3

Open the print software

  1. Open the DPCS folder at the top of the desktop.
  2. Open the DPCS Printer software inside it.
4

Temperature to 31 °C

  1. Go to Settings → Voltage.
  2. Set the temperature (Tempe. Set) to 31 °C.
  3. The printer must reach around 31 °C before you print. Meanwhile, continue with step 5.
5

Open the ink valves

  1. Take the key/rod.
  2. Turn only the valves that are connected (with a hose) a quarter turn back. Including white (W).
  3. Skip any valve that is not connected.
  4. Work along each colour from left to right: skip one, turn one.
6

Purge the print heads

  1. Take a non-dust cloth and fold it neatly.
  2. Press COLOR for 5 seconds.
  3. Press WHITE for 5 seconds.
  4. Check that the colour and the white run out.
7

Wipe the print head clean

  1. Wipe the print head clean with the folded cloth.
✓ Non-dust cloth, neatly folded
✗ Do not wipe back and forth

✓ Ready to print.

Checklist

All checked. The mug printer is ready.
