Shutting down the mug printer

At the end of the day. Shutting down the mug printer (Mug 1) is simple: valves closed, STOP and computer off.

1

Close the ink valves

  1. Turn the connected valves back down: the same valves you opened at startup.
2

Switch off the machine

  1. Press the red STOP button on the panel.
3

Shut down the computer

  1. Shut down the computer.

Checklist

Done. The mug printer is off.
