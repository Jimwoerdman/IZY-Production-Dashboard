Printing bottles on the bottle printer

How to print an order on Bottle 1 or Bottle 2. Do part 1 first: starting the printer.

1

Check the temperature

  1. In the software, check Temp.
  2. The printer is warm enough from 31 °C, up to about 34–35 °C.
2

Fill the rack

Do this while the printer is warming up.

  1. Take the cap off the bottle.
  2. Remove the packaging.
  3. Put the caps in the bin.
  4. Place the bottles in the rack.
3

Close the door and switch on extraction

  1. Close the printer door.
  2. Switch on the extraction: press the button at the top right.
4

Pick the print file

  1. Go to the print software and click the printer icon.
  2. Pick the print you are going to print from the list at the bottom (for example IZY Bottle TU DELFT).
  3. Tick Manual and High Drop.
  4. Click Modify.
5

Check the settings

Always check this:

  1. Zone Print is on.
  2. X Length is set to 238.
  3. Under Print Mode Setting: White/Varnish Mode is set to C and W.
  4. Click Apply.
6

Load the bottle

  1. Place the bottle in the machine while the clamp is off (retracted).
  2. Is the bottle in place? Press Push. The bottle is pushed forward and is now clamped.
  3. Then press Clamp again, so the clamp and the bottle are apart.
7

Print

  1. In the software, click Print at the top.
  2. At Print confirm, click Yes.
  3. The bottle is printed.
8

Bottle out, next one in

As soon as the bottle comes out of the printer, do the reverse:

  1. Press Clamp: the clamps retract again.
  2. Press Push: the bottle comes out again.
  3. Replace the bottle with the next one.
  4. Press Push, then Clamp.
  5. Start the next print as in step 7.
Remember the order

In: Push → Clamp. Out: Clamp → Push.

Checklist

All checked. The order is running.
