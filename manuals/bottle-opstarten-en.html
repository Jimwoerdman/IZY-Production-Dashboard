IZY Bottles · Work instruction · Part 1

Starting the bottle printer

How to switch on the bottle printer and get it ready to print. Applies to Bottle 1 and Bottle 2: they are the same printers. Do the steps for both machines.

You need: non-dust cloth (lint-free) · PC code: 1997

The machine

  • Power on button on the panel next to the tray, with the red emergency stop.
  • Behind the door: ink cartridges with valves and the catch tray.
  • In the left corner: the Color and White buttons.
  • Computer with the DPCS Printer software, and a compressor.
1

Switch on the machine and computer

  1. Press Power on.
  2. Switch on the computer.
2

Computer and software

  1. Log in to the computer with code 1997.
  2. Open the DPCS Printer folder at the top left of the desktop.
  3. Open the DPCS Printer software inside it.
3

Open the machine

  1. Open the machine door.
  2. You see the ink cartridges with the catch tray in the middle.
4

Open the ink valves

  1. Turn the valves a quarter turn down.
  2. Only do this for the middle 6: the valves that are connected.
  3. Leave the outer 2 as they are.
5

Purge the print heads

  1. The Color and White buttons are in the left corner.
  2. Press Color for 5 seconds: all colours come out.
  3. Press White for 5 seconds: the white comes out.
  4. Check very carefully that ink really comes out of all 4 stripes.
Not ink everywhere?

Press longer. Sometimes clean the print head extra (step 6) and do it again.

6

Wipe the print head clean

  1. Take a non-dust cloth and fold it neatly.
  2. Wipe the print head from front to back. Not back and forth.
✓ Non-dust cloth, neatly folded
✗ Do not wipe back and forth
7

Tray back and door closed

  1. Take out the catch tray and put it back in the corner.
  2. Close the door.
  3. Tidy up your workspace.
8

Switch on the compressor

Do not forget this on this printer!

  1. Turn the compressor knob to Auto.
  2. You hear the compressor start.
9

Check the temperature

  1. In the software, check the temperature (Temp column).
  2. It must be around 30 °C. Then you can start printing.
Two printers

Do steps 1 to 9 for the other machine as well (Bottle 1 and Bottle 2).

✓ Ready to print.

Checklist

All checked. The printers are ready.
